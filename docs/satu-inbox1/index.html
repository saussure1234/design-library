Invisible

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which channels can I connect?

WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok Shop, Shopee and Tokopedia chat. WhatsApp uses the official WhatsApp Cloud API from Meta, which needs a verified Meta business and a dedicated number. Instagram needs a Professional account. We help you through each step during onboarding.

Will the AI make up prices or promises?

No. Prices and stock come only from your product catalog, and order totals are calculated by the system, not by the AI. Complaints, refunds and discount requests are handed straight to a person.

Do I have to let the AI reply on its own?

No. You choose per channel: off, suggest (the AI drafts and your agent sends) or auto. We recommend starting in suggest mode and switching on auto only where you're comfortable.

Do I need my own AI key?

AI features run on your own key from Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI or Google (Gemini), so usage is billed to your account with that provider. Keys are encrypted before they're stored. If you don't have one yet, we can start you on ours during onboarding.

How is my customers' data handled?

Chat content is read by the AI provider you choose. We put how data is processed in writing, in line with Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (UU PDP), before you go live.

What happens after the free trial?

At the end of the 7 days you decide whether to continue, on the monthly or the yearly plan. The monthly plan can be cancelled anytime.
