      Question design

      We do not invent the questions. We start from what people type into Google, add the follow-up questions Google shows under "People also ask", then rewrite them as conversational questions for AI.

      1Google keywords

      What people type when looking for your type of business.

      2People also ask

      Follow-up questions from Google results. These are examples; during measurement they are collected from Google directly.

        3Questions for AI

        Rewritten as conversational questions and split into 3 layers.

        How to read the results

        • Layers are never added together. Layer 3 questions contain your name, so you always appear. Merging them would make the numbers look better than they are.
        • AI answers change from run to run. Each question is asked 3 times and we report "appeared 2 of 3 times", not just yes or no.
        • These are AI answers to questions we designed. We do not know exactly what your customers type, so the question list can be revised after the first measurement.
