From results to fixes

We don't stop at a score. We show what is missing, and why.

For each thing you want AI to say about you, such as your strengths, menu, area and price, we check two things: is it written somewhere AI reads, and does it come through in AI's answers?

  • Diagnosis

    What it means

    What to do

  • Working

    It is written, AI cites it, and it comes through in the answer.

    Keep it. We check it again every month.

  • Read, but the point is buried

    AI cites your page, but the point does not reach the answer.

    Say it clearly on that page: in a heading, the menu or an FAQ.

  • Written, but not picked up

    It is written, but only where AI does not look for you.

    Write the same thing where AI already reads: Google Maps, marketplace pages, reviews or the pages AI cites.

  • Not written anywhere

    It is true, but it is not online. AI cannot say it.

    Write it: a section on your page, an FAQ, your Maps description or a post.

  • Wrong

    AI says something untrue about you.

    We find the page it came from, so you can fix it at the source.
